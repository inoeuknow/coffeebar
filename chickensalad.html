Classic Keto Chicken Salad

Component Mass Calibration
Ingredient Mass Analytical Protocol Note
Chicken Breast 567 grams Pre-cooked and fully chilled; shredded or diced into uniform bite-sized pieces.
Cheddar Cheese 100 grams Dense lipid/protein block; cut into sharp, uniform cubes for structured mouthfeel.
Fuji Apple 150 grams Cored and finely diced; adds crisp structural crunch and controlled sweet acidity.
Chopped Pecans 60 grams Toasted lipid solids; coarse chop introduces rich textural contrast across matrix.
Celery Stalk 50 grams Finely diced; provides aqueous crunch and fresh vegetal backbone without excess moisture.
Red Onion 30 grams Finely minced; sharp allium bite to cut through dressing fat profile.

Dijon Mustard 5 grams Secondary emulsifier and acid source; sharpens lipid heavy palate.

Standard Operating Procedure
1. Base Isolation & Sizing: Weigh and shred 567 grams of pre-cooked, completely chilled chicken breast into uniform pieces. Transfer directly into a clean, chilled stainless steel mixing vessel.
2. Structural Solid Integration: Precision dice 150 grams of Fuji apple, 50 grams of celery stalk, and 30 grams of red onion into uniform small dice. Deposit into the mixing vessel alongside 100 grams of cubed cheddar cheese and 60 grams of chopped pecans.
3. Emulsion Matrix Preparation: In an auxiliary vessel, whisk 120 grams of full-fat mayonnaise with 5 grams of Dijon mustard, salt, and freshly cracked black pepper until the dressing phase is completely homogenous and uniform.
4. Suspension & Low-Shear Fold: Pour the emulsified dressing directly over the combined solid inclusions. Utilizing a wide silicone spatula, execute a gentle bottom-to-top fold to thoroughly coat every component without shearing the apple cell walls or softening the pecans.
5. Equilibration & Cold Storage: Transfer the blended chicken salad into an airtight glass storage container. Allow the matrix to rest refrigerated at or below 40°F (4°C) for a minimum of 30 minutes to equilibrate the aromatic profile prior to portion deployment. Stable for 4 days.
Storage Protocol: Refrigerate Immediately at ≤ 40°F
Fresh Matrix Window: Consume within 4 days
Deployment: Cold / Bowl or Wrap
