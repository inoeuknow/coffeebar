Specification Summary

Pre-chilling cooked chicken breast prevents lipid separation when folded into the Dijon-mayonnaise emulsion. Uniform small-dice Fuji apple, celery, and red onion preserve cellular crunch across a 4-day refrigerated hold window (approx. 4.2 cups total batch volume).

Chef's Analytical Notes
  • Storage & thermal protocol: Transfer immediately to a glass airtight lock container and hold refrigerated at or below 4°C (40°F); consume within 4 days for peak crispness.
  • Low-shear folding: Fold dressing from bottom to top with a wide silicone spatula to coat all solids without bruising Fuji apple cell walls or softening toasted pecans.
  • Deployment formats: Serve chilled at 240 grams per portion in a bowl or wrapped in crisp butter lettuce leaves.
