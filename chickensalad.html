Base & Solids

  • Full-fat mayonnaise 120 grams

Standard Preparation Protocol
01
Base Prep: Shred or dice 567 grams of pre-cooked, chilled chicken breast into uniform bite-sized pieces. Transfer into a high-capacity stainless steel or glass mixing bowl.
02
Solid Integration: Finely dice 150 grams of Fuji apple, 50 grams of celery stalk, and 30 grams of red onion. Add the diced produce, 100 grams of cheddar cheese cubes, and 60 grams of chopped pecans directly into the bowl with the prepared chicken.
03
Dressing Emulsion: In a separate vessel, whisk together 120 grams of full-fat mayonnaise, 5 milliliters of Dijon mustard, salt, and freshly cracked black pepper until homogenous and completely emulsified.
04
Uniform Fold: Pour the seasoned dressing mixture over the dry bowl contents. Using a silicone spatula, gently fold from bottom to top until the chicken, cheese cubes, and diced aromatics are evenly coated without crushing the apple structure.
05
Equilibration & Storage: Sample for seasoning balance. Seal container hermetically and refrigerate at or below 4 degrees Celsius for a minimum of 30 minutes to equilibrate flavors. Stable under refrigeration for up to 4 days.
