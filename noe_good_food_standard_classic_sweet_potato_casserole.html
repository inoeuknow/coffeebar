Classic Sweet Potato Casserole

Creamy roasted sweet potato custard baked under a crisp, buttery pecan and brown sugar crumble.

SPECIMEN FIG. 01 — ROASTED SWEET POTATO & STREUSEL
Active Preparation 30 minutes
Thermal Cook 35 minutes
Resting Window 15 minutes
Base Yield 10 servings
Process Envelope 190°C (375°F) bake
CASSEROLE SWEET POTATO PECAN CRUMBLE BAKED HOLIDAY SIDE
Specification Summary

Roasting whole sweet potatoes concentrates natural sugars and yields a smooth, velvety base without excess waterlogging. A 15-minute post-bake rest firms the custard and sets the toasted pecan streusel.
