Magnolia Bakery Classic Banana Pudding

No-bake vanilla custard folded with stiff-peaked heavy cream, ripe sliced bananas, and softened vanilla wafers.

SPECIMEN FIG. 01 — CHILLED CUSTARD & WAFER MATRIX
Prep Time 15 minutes
Chill Time 4 hours plus
Initial Set 3 to 4 hours
Base Yield 10 servings
Process Envelope chilled custard & layering
NO-BAKE BANANA PUDDING VANILLA CUSTARD CHILLED DESSERT VANILLA WAFERS
Specification Summary

Chilling the condensed milk and instant vanilla pudding base for at least 3 to 4 hours before folding into stiff-peaked heavy cream produces an airy, scoopable custard. A final 4-hour refrigeration rest hydrates the vanilla wafers into a tender, cake-like crumb.
