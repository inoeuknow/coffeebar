Standard Operating Procedure

1. Spice Decoction: Combine the 237 grams of water, 192 grams of allulose golden, thin-sliced ginger, cracked cinnamon, star anise, cloves, and smashed cardamom pods in a saucepan. Bring to a low, steady simmer. Secure a tight-fitting **Lid On** the vessel immediately to prevent solvent evaporation. Maintain this decoction phase for exactly 20 minutes.
2. Thermal Descent: Remove the saucepan from the heat source. Do not open the lid. Monitor the liquid temperature until it reaches exactly **195°F**. Transfer the hot liquid and all suspended botanical elements directly into an unheated French press carafe.
3. Controlled Extraction: Introduce the 28 grams of loose black tea into the 195°F botanical matrix. Cover with the French press lid (plunger fully raised) and allow to steep undisturbed for exactly 6 minutes. This temperature constraint safeguards against the extraction of heavy, bitter tannins that trigger clouding.
4. Mechanical Recovery: Depress the French press plunger slowly and smoothly until it meets the solid bed. Apply firm, steady downward force to compress the sodden tea leaves and ginger fibers. This mechanical compression forces out the concentrated micro-liquor trapped in the botanical cells, ensuring you reach the 14 oz target.
5. Packaging & Equilibration: Decant the recovered concentrate through a small funnel into a clean 16.9 fluid ounce pump bottle. Allow the container to reach room temperature natively on the bench before securing the pump mechanism and transferring it to cold storage.
