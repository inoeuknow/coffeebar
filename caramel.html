Caramel Master Sauce

Component Mass Calibration

Ingredient Mass Analytical Protocol Note
Allulose Golden 192 grams Provides color density; highly reactive to prolonged thermal curves.
Salted Butter 85 grams Source of intact milk lipids and proteins required for the Maillard reaction.
Heavy Cream 180 grams Must be pre-warmed to equal or greater than 100°F to prevent structural seizing.
Xanthan Gum 1 gram Hydrocolloid agent; binds distinct lipid and aqueous phases into a permanent matrix.

Standard Operating Procedure

1. Maillard Isolation: Melt the salted butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat. The lipid matrix will foam, then crackle loudly as water content evaporates. Continuously observe the vessel floor. The moment all physical sizzling ceases, tiny brown specks of toasted milk solids will precipitate, accompanied by a distinct hazelnut aroma. Immediately adjust heat to low.
2. Sweetener Integration: Add the allulose golden directly into the hot browned butter fat phase. Whisk continuously. The sweetener will melt and unify into a smooth, copper-toned syrup almost instantly. Do not let the mixture deepen further on the heat source; over-exposure at this step will generate an undesirable bitter molasses profile.
3. Thermal Quench: Terminate the burner heat completely. Immediately begin introducing the pre-warmed heavy cream in a slow, steady stream while whisking aggressively. The solution will undergo violent bubbling and steam release. This structural thermal quench drop-locks the flavor molecules and completely halts the chemical breakdown of the sweetener.
4. Hydrocolloid Stabilization: While the emulsion temperature remains between 150°F and 180°F, evenly dust the xanthan gum over the surface. Whisk vigorously for sixty seconds. A distinct mechanical drag will manifest as the polymer network hydrates, locking the fat and water into a stable emulsion.
5. Equilibration & Storage: Allow the completed sauce to drop to room temperature natively within the pan. Pour into an empty 16.9 fluid ounce squeeze bottle. The remaining 2.9 fluid ounces of volumetric headspace is calculated to prevent overflow during deployment. Store under refrigeration.
