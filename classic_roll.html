Preparation Steps

Numbered Protocol
01
Procedure Step

Prepare Pre-Ferments (Night Before): Tangzhong: Whisk 60 grams bread flour and 75 grams boiling water until a smooth paste forms; cool to ambient room temperature, cover tightly, and refrigerate overnight (8 to 12 hours). Poolish: Combine 50 grams bread flour, 50 grams room-temperature water, and 0.50 grams instant yeast; cover loosely, ferment at room temperature for 1 hour, then seal tightly and chill overnight (8 to 12 hours).

02
Procedure Step

Mix the Dough: In a stand mixer bowl, combine the chilled Tangzhong (straight from the refrigerator), chilled Poolish (straight from the refrigerator), 55 grams whole milk, 50 grams whole egg, 18 grams granulated sugar, and 5 grams fine salt. Mix on low speed until homogeneous.

03
Procedure Step

Add Flour and Knead (Autolyse): Add the remaining 190 grams bread flour. Mix on low speed for 2 minutes until hydrated, then rest the dough covered for 10 minutes (autolyse). Knead on medium-low speed for 5 to 7 minutes until smooth.

04
Procedure Step

Incorporate Butter to Windowpane: Add 20 grams softened unsalted butter one small piece at a time, mixing until fully absorbed after each addition. Continue kneading for 5 to 10 minutes until the dough is silky, elastic, and stretches into a thin translucent windowpane without tearing.

05
Procedure Step

First Rise (Bulk Fermentation): Transfer dough to a lightly oiled bowl and cover. For Same-Day Rise: Ferment in a warm environment for 1.5 to 2 hours until doubled in volume. For Cold Rise (Overnight Flavor): Cover tightly and refrigerate for 8 to 12 hours for slow enzymatic fermentation.

06
Procedure Step

Temper, Pre-Shape & Final Shape: If cold-risen, temper covered at room temperature for 45 to 60 minutes. Deflate gently and divide into 8 equal portions (approx. 72 grams each). Round into rough balls, cover, and rest on the bench for 15 minutes. Flatten each rested ball into a disk roughly 9 centimeters (3.5 inches) wide, keeping the center slightly thicker than the edges. Place 1 frozen 8-gram salted butter cube in the center, pull the edges up over the butter, and pinch the seam tightly at the bottom. Arrange seam-side down and spaced at least 5 centimeters (2 inches) apart on a parchment-lined baking sheet.

07
Procedure Step

Second Rise, Egg Wash & Bake: Cover loosely and proof in a warm spot for 45 to 60 minutes until puffy. Whisk 1 whole large egg with 1 teaspoon whole milk, brush gently over the proofed tops, and sprinkle 5 grams flaky sea salt over the center of each crown. Bake in a preheated 200°C (400°F) oven for 14 to 17 minutes until the tops are deep golden brown and the bottoms are crisp and butter-fried. Cool on the sheet pan for 3 to 5 minutes before serving so the crisp bottom sets.

Chef's Analytical Notes
  • Pre-ferment temperature control: Incorporate both the Tangzhong and Poolish directly from refrigeration to moderate dough friction temperature during extended stand-mixer kneading.
  • Bench rest relaxation: The 15-minute rest after dividing into 72-gram portions relaxes gluten tension so final shaping forms tight, smooth surface tension without tearing.
  • Windowpane verification: Knead after butter incorporation until a small dough sample stretches into a translucent membrane without breaking.
