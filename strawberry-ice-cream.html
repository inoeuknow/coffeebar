2.0 Standard Operating Procedure
  • 2.1
    Thermal Reduction: Puree two hundred and fifty grams of fresh strawberries. Simmer over medium-low heat in a small saucepan, stirring constantly, until reduced to a thick jam-like paste weighing exactly sixty grams. Cool completely.
  • 2.2
    Dry Pre-Dispersion: Whisk ninety grams of Lakanto Sweetener and two grams of xanthan gum together in a dry mixing vessel to guarantee uniform hydrocolloid dispersion.
  • 2.3
    Homogenization: Introduce two hundred and forty grams of Fairlife milk, two hundred and twenty grams of heavy cream, and sixty grams of cooled strawberry puree. Apply high shear via immersion blender for ninety seconds until emulsified.
  • 2.4
    Primary Freezing (Soft Serve): Transfer mixture to the Gourmia internal chamber. Run the standard preset automated freezing cycle until ideal soft-serve peaks are achieved (approximately twenty-five to thirty minutes).
  • 2.5
    Cryogenic Recovery Protocol: Transfer unconsumed soft serve into a standard Ninja Creami pint. Level the top surface perfectly flat. Freeze at negative eighteen degrees Celsius for twenty-four hours. Process on 'Lite Ice Cream' followed by one optional milk 'Re-Spin' if texturally required.
