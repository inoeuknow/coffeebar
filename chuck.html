Standard Operating Procedure

1. Surface Seasoning & Capture: Prepare the custom dry surface agent by blending 20.6 grams of coarse kosher salt, 20.6 grams of 16-mesh black pepper, 10.3 grams of granulated garlic, 5.2 grams of granulated onion, and 2.6 grams of monosodium glutamate to complete homogeneity. Apply all 56.7 grams of the rub uniformly to all surfaces. Vacuum seal immediately to prevent fluid loss.
2. Thermal Equilibrium Bath: Submerge the sealed package fully into a water bath stabilized at 155°F. Shield the upper surface of the bath container to manage volatile vapor losses. Maintain this precise thermal cook for a duration of 24 hours to hydrolyze the collagen.
3. Core Cryo-Shocking: Extract the pack cleanly from the water bath and plunge it directly into an ice-water slurry for exactly 60 minutes. This critical thermal boundary prevents internal over-extraction and graying during the subsequent smoking loop, shielding the delicate core structure.
4. Pyrolytic Smoke Phase: Light the smoker using seasoned oak or hickory and stabilize the atmosphere at 225°F. Strip the roast from the bag, reserving all pure bag liquor for subsequent glaze reduction. Dry the damp meat skin to a complete matte finish using clean paper towels. Dust with a tiny, secondary dusting of rub. Smoke for 2 to 2.5 hours until a dark, crisp bark sets.
5. Shear Recovery & Service: Simmer the reserved bag juices, skimming the surface proteins. Whisk in 1 tablespoon of cold butter to emulsify. Rest the smoked roast for 10 minutes, then shear cleanly against the muscle grain into 1/4-inch segments. Drizzle with the warm reduction and serve immediately.
